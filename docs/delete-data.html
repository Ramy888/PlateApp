Delete your data

Last updated 6 September 2026

PlatePatch has no accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server holding your information — so there is no account for us to delete. Everything PlatePatch knows about you is stored on your own phone.

Delete everything, right now

Two options, both instant and neither of which needs us:

  • Delete individual saved patches — open Saved patches in the app and tap the × on any entry.
  • Delete all of it — uninstall PlatePatch. Android and iOS remove the app's entire storage with it. Nothing survives the uninstall, and nothing is kept anywhere else.

What is stored, and where

DataWhere it livesHow to delete it
Your goal and dietary preferencesYour device onlyUninstall the app
Saved patches and after-meal answersYour device onlyTap × in the app, or uninstall
Purchase record and an anonymous device identifierRevenueCat, our subscription processorEmail us — see below

The purchase record exists so a subscription can be restored on a new phone. It contains no meal data, no preferences, no health information, and no name or email address.

Requesting deletion of purchase data

If you have subscribed and want the purchase record removed, email support@platepatch.app with the subject “Data deletion request”, and include the email address you used on Google Play or the App Store so the record can be found.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. Deleting the purchase record ends the ability to restore that subscription on a new device, so cancel the subscription in your app store first if you no longer want it.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling and deleting are separate. To cancel, use Google Play subscriptions or App Store subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; your current period runs to its end.
