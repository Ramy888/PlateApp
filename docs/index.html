PlatePatch

Add one thing to the meal you already have.

PlatePatch answers a single question: what small addition would make this meal more satisfying? No calorie counting, no weighing, no logging, no guilt.

How it works

Tell it whether it is breakfast, lunch or dinner, or a snack. Tap what is on the plate. PlatePatch works out what the meal is light on — protein, fibre, or healthy fats — and gives you three ways to fix it: the fastest, the cheapest, and a plant-based one.

Afterwards it asks one question: still hungry, comfortably satisfied, or too full? That single answer is the only thing it learns from.

What it is not

Not a calorie tracker. Not a diet. Not a food diary. Not medical advice — see the terms.

Privacy

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything you tap stays on your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
